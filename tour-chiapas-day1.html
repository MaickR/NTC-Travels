Complete 5-Day Itinerary

Day 1
March 3

Airport Reception & San Cristóbal Arrival

Full Day Program

Highlights:

  • Reception at Tuxtla Gutiérrez International Airport - Welcome meeting with bilingual guide
  • 1-Hour Scenic Transfer to San Cristóbal de las Casas (2,120m elevation)
  • Hotel Check-in: Villa Mercedes (4-star) at 14:00 hrs
  • Welcome Briefing - Overview of 5-day adventure
  • Afternoon Rest - Acclimatization to altitude
  • Evening: Welcome dinner at local restaurant, group orientation

Meals: Lunch & Dinner included | Hotel: Villa Mercedes

Day 2
March 4

Chiflón Waterfalls & Montebello Lakes

08:00 AM - 21:00 (9 hours)

Highlights:

  • 08:00 AM: Hotel departure with packed breakfast
  • Cascadas El Chiflón: Five spectacular turquoise waterfalls with 1.2km scenic walk
  • Swimming Time: Swim in crystal-clear natural pools (bring swimsuit!)
  • Montebello National Park: Visit 5 stunning alpine lakes surrounded by pine forests
  • Photography Opportunities: Capture breathtaking panoramic views
  • Lunch: At local restaurant (included)
  • 21:00 PM: Return to hotel and rest

Meals: Breakfast & Lunch included | Activity Type: Shared Service Tour | What to Bring: Swimsuit, water shoes, sunscreen, camera

Day 3
March 5

Sumidero Canyon & Chiapa de Corzo Colonial Town

09:00 AM - 18:00 (9 hours)

Highlights:

  • 09:00 AM: Departure with hotel breakfast pack
  • Cañón del Sumidero: UNESCO-recognized natural wonder with 1,000m-high cliffs
  • 2-Hour Boat Tour: Navigate through spectacular canyon formations and spot local wildlife
  • Chiapa de Corzo Colonial Town: Walk through centuries-old architecture and cobblestone streets
  • Local Market Exploration: Discover authentic crafts and traditional goods
  • Panoramic Viewpoints (Miradores): Hike to canyon overlooks for stunning photography
  • Lunch: At local restaurant overlooking the canyon (included)
  • 18:00 PM: Return to hotel

Meals: Breakfast & Lunch included | Activity Type: Shared Service Tour | Physical Level: Moderate (boat tour + light hiking)

Day 4
March 6

Agua Azul, Misol-Ha & Palenque Archaeological Zone

04:30 AM - 17:00 (12+ hours) - FULL DAY ADVENTURE

Highlights:

  • 04:30 AM: Early departure (continental breakfast included)
  • Cascadas de Agua Azul: Marvel at 500+ turquoise waterfalls cascading through jungle (1.5 hours)
  • Swimming Break: Swim in natural pools of breathtaking blue water
  • Misol-Ha Waterfall: Explore the spectacular 35m waterfall with optional cave visit (1 hour)
  • Lunch: Included at local restaurant
  • Palenque Archaeological Zone (UNESCO): 2-hour guided exploration of this magnificent Mayan ruin complex
  • Key Structures: Temple of the Inscriptions, Palace Complex, Queen's Bath, and more
  • Expert Archaeological Guide: Learn about Mayan civilization dating back 1,500+ years
  • 17:00 PM: Return to hotel after one of the most remarkable days of your life

Meals: Continental Breakfast & Lunch included | Activity Type: Shared Service Tour | What to Bring: Swimsuit, sturdy hiking boots, water shoes, sunscreen, bug spray, camera | Physical Level: Moderate to Challenging

Day 5
March 7

Transfer to Mexico-Guatemala Border (El Ceibo)

Full Day Program

Highlights:

  • Hotel Breakfast: Final meal at Villa Mercedes
  • Scenic Drive: Through lush jungle and coffee plantations towards Guatemala border
  • El Ceibo Border Crossing: Experience the authentic Mexico-Guatemala frontier
  • Border Experience: Visit the tri-border area where Mexico, Guatemala, and Belize meet
  • Local Markets: Explore border town commerce and culture
  • Return Transfer: Back to Tuxtla Gutiérrez airport or hotel
  • Certificate of Completion: Receive your Chiapas Adventure certificate
  • Final Farewell: Goodbye reception and safe travels

Meals: Breakfast included | End Time: Approximately 18:00 PM

Package Includes & Important Details

What's Included
  • Hotel: 1 night at Villa Mercedes (4-star)
  • Transportación: Round-trip airport transfers + tour transportation
  • Meals: Daily breakfasts (hotel + packed) + 4 lunches
  • Park Entries: CONANP (National Parks) & INAH (Archaeological Sites) fees
  • Boat Tours: Sumidero Canyon boat tour (2 hours)
  • Guided Tours: Expert bilingual guides throughout
  • Travel Insurance: Full coverage included
  • Group Experience: Shared services with 22+ people minimum
NOT Included
  • Dinner meals (except Day 1 welcome dinner)
  • Personal expenses and souvenirs
  • Travel to Tuxtla Gutiérrez airport
  • Optional activities and excursions
  • Beverages with meals
  • Tips and gratuities (recommended 5-10%)
  • International flight tickets
Important Information
  • Minimum Group: 22 people
  • Accessibility: Tours include moderate physical activity
  • Languages: Spanish & English guides available
  • Age Requirements: No age restrictions (families welcome)
  • Payment: Installment plans available (4 monthly payments)
  • Cancellation: 30-day cancellation policy applies
Packing Recommendations
  • Swimwear: Swimsuit & water shoes (4 water activities)
  • Footwear: Sturdy hiking boots for Day 3-4
  • Sun Protection: High SPF sunscreen, hat, sunglasses
  • Clothing: Light layers for temperature changes (altitude)
  • Insect Protection: Mosquito repellent with DEET
  • Medications: Personal medications + altitude sickness remedies
  • Camera: Waterproof recommended for water activities
Pro Tips for Your Adventure
  • Altitude: San Cristóbal is at 2,120m. Drink plenty of water and rest on Day 1 to acclimate.
  • Best Photography: Days 2 and 4 offer the most spectacular photo opportunities - bring extra memory cards!
  • Currency: Mexican Pesos recommended. USD also accepted but at unfavorable rates.
  • Travel Insurance: While included, consider upgrading if you have pre-existing conditions.
  • Border Crossing: Bring valid passport for El Ceibo border experience on Day 5.
