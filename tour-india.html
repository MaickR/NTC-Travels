Itinerary: 12 Days / 11 Nights

Route: Delhi (2N) → Jaipur (2N) → Agra (2N) → Varanasi (2N) → Rishikesh (3N) → Delhi

1
Arrival

Arrival in Delhi

Welcome to India! The capital of India, Delhi, is one of the most populous cities in the world. It is a bustling and multifaceted city with a long history that serves as the main political, cultural, and economic center of India.

Arrive at Delhi International Airport and be greeted by our guest ambassador at the airport, who will be holding a placard with your name. Our guest ambassador will welcome you by offering a garland with a silk scarf. After the welcome, our guest ambassador will accompany you to your designated hotel.

Upon arrival at the hotel, you will be greeted with a ceremony called Arti and tikka and then complete the standard check-in formalities for room allocation.

Hotel: Le Meridian, New Delhi (Executive Room) | Meals: Breakfast

2
Delhi

In Delhi

Breakfast at the hotel. Meet your tour guide in the lobby and start the day with a tour of Old Delhi. Pure indulgence and immersion in the local cultural flavors of a beautiful historical city. The city of Shahjahanabad, built by the Mughals.

  • Jama Masjid: One of the largest mosques in India. Located opposite the Red Fort.
  • Rickshaw Ride: Adventure through the narrow streets and ancient markets of Old Delhi.
  • Red Fort (Photo Stop): Also known as Lal Qila, a historic fortification.
  • Raj Ghat: Memorial dedicated to Mahatma Gandhi.
  • Gurdwara Bangla Sahib: Sikh temple with its community kitchen serving thousands daily.

Afternoon visit to New Delhi: Designed by the British in 1920. Visit Qutub Minar (UNESCO World Heritage), Humayun's Tomb (UNESCO World Heritage), and drive past India Gate, Parliament House, and the President's Residence. Later, free time for shopping at Khan Market.

Hotel: Le Meridian, New Delhi (Executive Room) | Meals: Breakfast

3
Jaipur

Delhi – Jaipur (4-5 hours)

Breakfast at the hotel and check-out. Today you will drive to Jaipur, also known as the Pink City, Rajasthan. The vibrant capital of Rajasthan is popularly known as the "Pink City" due to the pink buildings in its old town.

Arrival in Jaipur and check-in at your hotel. A non-alcoholic welcome drink will be served to all guests. In the late afternoon, your guide will take you to a local Hindu temple to witness the live aarti ceremony.

Hotel: ITC Rajputana, Jaipur (Executive Club) | Meals: Breakfast

4
Jaipur

In Jaipur

Breakfast at the hotel. Meet your guide in the lobby and begin your excursion to Amber Fort.

  • Amber Fort: A magnificent fortress located in Jaipur. Enjoy an Elephant or Jeep ride to ascend the hill. Blessing ceremony at the Hindu temple inside the Fort.
  • Photo stop at Jal Mahal Palace.
  • City Palace & Museum: A mix of Rajasthani and Mughal architecture.
  • Jantar Mantar Observatory: The largest and best preserved of the five observatories built by Jai Singh.

After the visits, explore the local market "Bapu Bazzar" and enjoy a Tuk-Tuk ride.

Hotel: ITC Rajputana, Jaipur (Executive Club) | Meals: Breakfast

5
Agra

Jaipur – Fatehpur Sikri – Agra (4-5 hours drive)

Breakfast at the hotel. Then drive towards Agra. En route, visit the beautiful ancient Mughal capital: Fatehpur Sikri. Explore the deserted red sandstone city built by Emperor Akbar.

Continue to Agra. Agra is the city of love and longing, meticulously planned by three successive Mughal emperors. Check in at the hotel. A non-alcoholic welcome drink will be served to all guests.

Hotel: ITC Mughal, Agra (Mughal Room) | Meals: Breakfast

6
Agra

Agra (Taj Mahal)

Early morning visit to the Taj Mahal. The magnificent monument to love, built by an emperor in memory of his beloved queen. Its marble exterior reflects pink and golden hues at sunrise. (Closed on Fridays).

Return to hotel for breakfast. Later proceed to visit Agra Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage site, also known as Lal Qila.

Hotel: ITC Mughal, Agra (Mughal Room) | Meals: Breakfast

7
Varanasi

Agra – Delhi (Drive) – Varanasi (Flight)

Breakfast at the hotel. Then travel to Delhi. Upon arrival, transfer to Delhi airport to board flight to Varanasi (Indigo 6E 2414, Dep 18:40).

Arrival in Varanasi at 19:55. Varanasi, also known as Kashi (City of Light), is one of the oldest inhabited cities in the world. Transfer to hotel for check-in. A special traditional Indian welcome awaits you.

Hotel: Taj Ganges, Varanasi (Superior Room) | Meals: Breakfast

8
Varanasi

Varanasi Sightseeing

Early morning Boat Ride on the Ganges followed by a walking tour through the narrow streets of Varanasi. Visit the Golden Temple (Kashi Vishwanath) and Bharat Mata Temple.

Excursion to Sarnath, where Lord Buddha preached his first sermon. Visit the Stupa and Museum.

In the late afternoon, proceed to witness the Ganga Aarti ceremony on the ghats. The lamps are swirled around the idols accompanied by chanting of hymns.

Hotel: Taj Ganges, Varanasi (Superior Room) | Meals: Breakfast

9
Rishikesh

Varanasi – Delhi (Flight) – Dehradun (Flight) – Rishikesh

Breakfast at the hotel. Transfer to Varanasi airport for flight to Delhi (Indigo 6E-2321, Dep 09:35). Connect to flight to Dehradun (Indigo 6E-2135, Dep 15:10).

Arrival in Dehradun at 15:55. Drive to Rishikesh (1.5 hours). Rishikesh is at the base of the Himalayas where the Ganges descends to the plains. Check-in at hotel.

Hotel: Divine Resort, Rishikesh (Standard Room) | Meals: Breakfast

10
Rishikesh

Rishikesh Sightseeing & Yoga

Morning Yoga and meditation session at the hotel. After breakfast, half-day tour of Rishikesh. Visit Triveni Ghat, Bharat Mandir, and Chandreshwar Temple. Visit Parmarth Ashram. In the evening, enjoy the aarti ceremony in Rishikesh.

Hotel: Divine Resort, Rishikesh (Standard Room) | Meals: Breakfast

11
Haridwar

Excursion to Haridwar

Morning Yoga and meditation session at the hotel. Breakfast at hotel. Afternoon drive to Haridwar (approx. 1.5 hours) for a day excursion including visit to Har-Ki-Paudi and witness the evening aarti ceremony on the banks of the holy river Ganges. Return to hotel.

Hotel: Divine Resort, Rishikesh (Standard Room) | Meals: Breakfast

12
Departure

Rishikesh – Dehradun – Delhi – Departure

After breakfast, drive to Dehradun in time to catch flight to Delhi (Air India AI-2908, Dep 13:40). Arrival in Delhi at 14:35. Transfer to International Airport to connect with flight to your next destination.

Meals: Breakfast
