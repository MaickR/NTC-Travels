Itinerary: 12 Days / 11 Nights

Route: Delhi (2N) → Jaipur (2N) → Agra (2N) → Varanasi (2N) → Rishikesh (3N) → Delhi

Day 1
Apr 22

Arrival in Delhi

Arrival at Delhi International Airport. You will be greeted by our Ambassador holding a placard with your name, who will welcome you with a traditional garland and silk scarf. Transfer to your hotel.

Upon arrival at the hotel, enjoy a traditional "Arti and Tikka" welcome ceremony. Check-in and rest.

Hotel: Le Meridian, New Delhi (Executive Room) | Meals: Breakfast

Day 2
Apr 23

Old & New Delhi Sightseeing

Morning (Old Delhi): Visit Jama Masjid, enjoy a thrilling rickshaw ride through Chandni Chowk and ancient markets. Photo stop at Red Fort. Visit Raj Ghat (Gandhi Memorial) and Gurdwara Bangla Sahib with its community kitchen (Langar).

Afternoon (New Delhi): Panoramic drive past India Gate, Parliament House, and President's Residence. Visit Qutub Minar and Humayun's Tomb. Free time for shopping at Khan Market.

Hotel: Le Meridian, New Delhi (Executive Room) | Meals: Breakfast

Day 3
Apr 24

Delhi – Jaipur (The Pink City)

Drive to Jaipur (approx. 5-6 hours). Arrival and welcome with a non-alcoholic drink. In the evening, visit a local Hindu temple to witness a live Aarti ceremony.

Hotel: ITC Rajputana, Jaipur (Executive Club) | Meals: Breakfast

Day 4
Apr 25

Jaipur Cultural Immersion

Ascend to Amber Fort by Jeep. Participate in a special Hindu blessing ceremony inside the fort. Photo stop at Jal Mahal (Water Palace).

Visit City Palace & Museum and Jantar Mantar Observatory. Enjoy a Tuk-Tuk ride and shopping at Bapu Bazaar.

Special Inclusions: Henna painting on hands and traditional Sari/Turban tying session.

Hotel: ITC Rajputana, Jaipur (Executive Club) | Meals: Breakfast

Day 5
Apr 26

Jaipur – Fatehpur Sikri – Agra

Drive to Agra. En route, visit the abandoned Mughal city of Fatehpur Sikri. Arrival in Agra and welcome drink.

Hotel: ITC Mughal, Agra (Mughal Room) | Meals: Breakfast

Day 6
Apr 27

Taj Mahal Sunrise & Agra Fort

Early morning visit to the Taj Mahal at sunrise (Battery bus included). Special Feature: Photo session with traditional Indian dress (Sari for women, Kurta-Pajama for men) + photo copy included.

Return to hotel for breakfast. Later, visit the majestic Agra Fort. Afternoon at leisure.

Hotel: ITC Mughal, Agra (Mughal Room) | Meals: Breakfast

Day 7
Apr 28

Agra – Delhi – Varanasi (Flight)

Drive to Delhi airport. Fly to Varanasi (Indigo 6E 2414, Dep 18:40 - Arr 19:55). Traditional welcome upon arrival and transfer to hotel.

Hotel: Taj Ganges, Varanasi (Superior Room) | Meals: Breakfast

Day 8
Apr 29

Spiritual Varanasi

Early morning boat ride on the Ganges and walking tour of ancient alleys. Visit Golden Temple (exterior) and Durga Temple. Excursion to Sarnath (Buddha's first sermon site).

Evening: Witness the mesmerizing Ganga Aarti ceremony on the ghats.

Hotel: Taj Ganges, Varanasi (Superior Room) | Meals: Breakfast

Day 9
Apr 30

Varanasi – Rishikesh (Flights)

Fly Varanasi to Delhi (Indigo 6E-2321, Dep 09:35), then connect to Dehradun (Indigo 6E-2135, Dep 15:10 - Arr 15:55). Drive to Rishikesh (1.5 hrs). Check-in.

Hotel: Divine Resort, Rishikesh (Standard Room) | Meals: Breakfast

Day 10
May 1

Yoga & Rishikesh Tour

Morning Yoga & Meditation session. Half-day tour: Triveni Ghat, Bharat Mandir, Parmarth Niketan Ashram, suspension bridges. Evening Aarti ceremony.

Hotel: Divine Resort, Rishikesh (Standard Room) | Meals: Breakfast

Day 11
May 2

Haridwar Excursion

Morning Yoga & Meditation. Free time for optional activities (rafting/trekking). Afternoon excursion to Haridwar to visit Har-Ki-Paudi and witness the evening Ganga Aarti. Return to Rishikesh.

Hotel: Divine Resort, Rishikesh (Standard Room) | Meals: Breakfast

Day 12
May 3

Departure

Transfer to Dehradun airport. Fly to Delhi (Air India AI-2908, Dep 13:40 - Arr 14:35). Transfer to International Airport for your onward journey.

Meals: Breakfast
