Andalucía Journey

Córdoba, Sevilla, Cádiz/Jerez, Málaga & Granada

May 5 - 16 | 12 Days / 11 Nights

Route: Córdoba → Sevilla → Puerto de Santa María → Málaga → Granada → Madrid

Why choose this trip to Andalucía?

Experience Southern Spain through its most iconic cities and traditions: historical neighborhoods, monumental architecture, local wine culture, and unforgettable evenings of flamenco.

This itinerary combines high-speed AVE train travel, private transfers where needed, and guided visits across Córdoba, Sevilla, the Cádiz bay area, Málaga, and Granada—finishing with the AVE to Madrid for international connections.

  • Córdoba: Judería, Alcázar gardens, and the Mezquita.
  • Sevilla: Real Alcázar, Cathedral & Giralda, Triana, and flamenco night.
  • Cádiz/Jerez area: bodegas, horse fair, and Cádiz bay navigation.
  • Málaga: Cathedral, Calle Larios, Picasso Museum and Alcazaba.
  • Granada: Capilla Real, Cathedral, Sacromonte night, Alhambra & Generalife.
Dates: May 5-16

Private transfer to Málaga with a stop in Ronda to see Puente Nuevo, scenic views over the Tajo river, and the famous bullring.
