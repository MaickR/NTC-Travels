7-Day Guatemala Adventure Itinerary

March 7-13, 2025 | Comprehensive exploration of ancient Maya civilization and colonial heritage

Day 1

March 7
Arrival in Flores
Welcome to Flores & Lake Petén Itzá

Arrive at Mundo Maya International Airport and transfer to Flores. Check into your hotel at Hotel Ramada. Enjoy a welcome dinner and relax by the beautiful Lake Petén Itzá, the heart of ancient Maya civilization.

Meals

Welcome dinner included

Accommodation

Hotel Ramada, Flores

Day 2

March 8
Tikal Exploration
Tikal National Park - UNESCO World Heritage Site

Full day exploration of Tikal, one of the largest archaeological sites in the Americas. Discover the ancient Maya pyramids, temples, and palaces that rise above the jungle canopy. Optional helicopter tour available for aerial views of the complex.

Highlights
  • • Temple IV (65m high)
  • • Great Plaza & Acropolis
  • • Wildlife observation
  • • Helicopter tour option (+$600)
Meals

Breakfast and picnic lunch included

Day 3

March 9
Yaxha & Ceremony
Yaxha Archaeological Site & Maya Ceremony

Travel to Yaxha National Park, a UNESCO site featuring 500 structures including pyramids and palaces. Participate in a traditional Maya ceremony led by local shaman Tara Julio, connecting with ancient spiritual traditions.

Activities
  • • Yaxha archaeological site
  • • Traditional Maya ceremony
  • • Lake Yaxha exploration
  • • Local guide Oscar
Meals

Breakfast and lunch included

Day 4

March 10
Transfer to Antigua
Journey to Antigua Guatemala

Private transfer from Flores to Antigua (approximately 6-7 hours). Cross the border into Guatemala and arrive in the colonial city of Antigua, a UNESCO World Heritage site known for its baroque architecture and colorful facades.

Transportation

Private van transfer with guide

Accommodation

Antigua hotel (TBD)

Day 5

March 11
Antigua Exploration
Antigua Cultural Immersion

Full day exploring Antigua's colonial architecture, churches, and cultural heritage. Visit the stunning Santa Maria Cathedral, La Merced Church, and wander the cobblestone streets of this beautifully preserved colonial city.

Highlights
  • • Santa Maria Cathedral
  • • La Merced Church
  • • Central Park & markets
  • • Colonial architecture tour
Meals

Breakfast and lunch included

Day 6

March 12
Antigua Free Day
Antigua Free Exploration

Free day in Antigua to explore at your own pace. Visit museums, take cooking classes, shop for handicrafts, or simply relax in the colonial atmosphere. Optional activities available for those interested in additional cultural experiences.

Optional Activities
  • • Chocolate making workshop
  • • Weaving demonstration
  • • Coffee plantation visit
  • • Local market exploration
Meals

Breakfast included

Day 7

March 13
Departure
Departure from Guatemala

Morning transfer to Guatemala City International Airport for your departure flight. Take with you unforgettable memories of ancient Maya civilization and colonial heritage. Safe travels!

Schedule

Airport transfer based on flight time

Meals

Breakfast included
