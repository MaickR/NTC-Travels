Experience the Legacy of NTC Vineyards

A private invitation to walk the soil of the historic NTC estates, guided by the owner himself.

Join Nicolas Tena for a once-in-a-lifetime immersion into the heart of Spanish wine culture. This is not just a tour; it is a private invitation to walk the soil of the historic NTC vineyards in Spain, guided by the owner himself.

Designed exclusively for an intimate group of 10 travelers, this expedition offers a unique cultural experience where you will taste exceptional wines, discover the secrets of winemaking from the roots to the cellar, indulge in curated gastronomy, and live an authentic cultural immersion in the Spanish wine country.

Trip Highlights

  • The Vineyards: Exclusive access to explore the historic NTC private estates, walking the very rows where our signature wines begin.
  • The Legacy: A masterclass in tradition. Learn the guarded secrets of winemaking, directly from the roots to the barrel cellar.
  • The Experience: A strictly limited, intimate group immersion. Designed for 10 people to ensure personal attention and a family-style atmosphere.
  • Gastronomy: Authentic tapas pairings and regional culinary delights hosted by Nicolas.

Tour Details & Pricing

  • Group Size: 10 travelers (group rate applies).
  • Hosted By: Nicolas Tena (Owner, NTC Vineyards).
  • Price: $3,950 USD for the entire group of 10 people.
  • Experience: Wine tastings, cultural immersion, and visits to NTC vineyards.
Inclusions
  • All land transportation within Spain during the tour.
